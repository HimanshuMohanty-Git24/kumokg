## When you need a commercial license

A paid KumoKG Commercial License is required for **any commercial use**, for example:

- **Internal company or enterprise workflows:** using KumoKG inside a for-profit company, including internal
  knowledge graphs, data pipelines, analytics, research and development, and employee tooling, even if
  nothing is sold.
- **Commercial products and SaaS:** shipping KumoKG, or graphs, exports or features built with it, as part
  of a paid product, hosted service, API or platform.
- **Paid consulting and agency work:** using KumoKG to produce deliverables for clients, or building client
  solutions on top of it.
- **Commercial distribution:** selling, bundling, sublicensing or redistributing KumoKG, or modified
  versions of it, as part of a commercial offering.

Not sure whether your use counts as commercial? Ask before you deploy; see [Contact](#contact).
